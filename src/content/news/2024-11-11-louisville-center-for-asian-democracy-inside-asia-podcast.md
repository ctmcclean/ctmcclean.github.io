I had a great time joining David Buckley’s Inside Asia podcast at the University of Louisville’s Center for Asian Democracy to [discuss the LDP's recent election performance](https://rss.com/podcasts/cadpodcast/1743017/)—and what the results reveal about youth participation and [representation](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11109-024-09920-2) in Japan's rapidly aging society. We also chatted about [snap elections](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0010414021997172)!
