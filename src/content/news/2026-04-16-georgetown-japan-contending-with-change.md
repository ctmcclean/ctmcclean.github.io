I joined Mari Miura (Sophia University) and Kay Shimizu (University of Pittsburgh) for a panel on the politics of social change at [Georgetown's](https://asianstudies.georgetown.edu/) conference on Japan, organized by Sheila Smith. I spoke about why Takaichi has drawn such strong support from Japan's youngest voters and what that means for the LDP.
